## EN: Do Your Sculptures Come with a Guarantee of Authenticity?

Yes, we provide a comprehensive guarantee of authenticity for all sculptures purchased from our collection. ## Referanslarınızdaki heykellerin telif hakları kime aittir?

Telif hakları konusu, projenin niteliğine ve sözleşme şartlarına göre netleştirilir. Genellikle, müşteriye özel olarak tasarlanan ve üretilen heykellerin maddi telif hakları (eserin çoğaltılması, satılması gibi) sözleşme ile müşteriye devredilir. Ancak, eserin yaratıcısı olarak Heykel.org manevi telif hakları (eserde adımızın geçmesi gibi) saklı kalır. Bu, sanatsal bütünlüğün korunması açısından önemlidir. Proje başlangıcında, telif haklarının devri ve kullanım şartları detaylı bir şekilde sözleşmeye eklenerek tam şeffaflık sağlanır.

## EN: Who owns the copyrights of the sculptures in your references?

Copyrights are clarified according to the nature of the project and the contract terms. Generally, the material copyrights (such as reproduction, sale of the work) of sculptures specially designed and produced for the client are transferred to the client via contract. However, Heykel.org moral rights (such as being credited as the creator) are reserved. This is important for preserving artistic integrity. At the start of the project, the transfer of copyrights and usage terms are detailed in the contract to ensure full transparency.

## EN: How many stages of quality control are there in the production process?

Our production process is managed with a six-stage quality control system. In the first stage, design and sketch approval, in the second stage, model production and measurement control are performed. In the third stage, raw material processing and form control are carried out. The fourth stage is surface quality and detail inspection after casting or shaping. In the fifth stage, the suitability of patina, painting or polishing processes is checked. In the final stage, overall aesthetic evaluation, durability tests and compliance with customer specifications are performed. No stage proceeds to the next without approval.

## Alçı fabrikası nasıl üretim yapar?

Alçı fabrikalarında üretim süreci ham jips taşının doğadan çıkarılmasıyla başlar. Ocaklardan elde edilen jips taşı önce kırıcılardan geçirilerek küçük parçalara ayrılır. Ardından yüksek ısılı fırınlarda pişirme işlemine tabi tutulur; bu aşamada taş bünyesindeki su molekülleri uzaklaştırılır. Pişirme sonrası elde edilen malzeme öğütülerek toz hale getirilir. Son aşamada kalite kontrol testlerinden geçirilen alçı tozu, kullanım amacına göre sınıflandırılarak paketlenir ve dağıtıma hazır hale gelir.

## EN: How does a plaster factory produce?

The production process in plaster factories begins with the extraction of raw gypsum stone from nature. Gypsum stone obtained from quarries is first passed through crushers and broken into small pieces. It is then subjected to a firing process in high-temperature kilns; at this stage, the water molecules within the stone are removed. The material obtained after firing is ground into powder form. In the final stage, the plaster powder, having passed quality control tests, is classified according to its intended use, packaged, and made ready for distribution.
